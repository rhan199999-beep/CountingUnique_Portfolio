# Brand Guidelines - Counting Unique

## Brand Voice

**Core Message:** I test crazy ideas on camera so you don't have to.

**Tone:** Playful, adventurous, authentic, experimental

**Key Phrases:**
- "Crazy ideas on camera"
- "Testing on camera so you don't have to"
- "From concept to creation"

**Brand Personality:**
- ✅ Playful experimentation
- ✅ Authentic trials and errors
- ✅ Results-oriented (what actually works)
- ✅ Visually distinctive (mint green, Bricolage Grotesque)

**Do Not Say:**
- ❌ "Lifestyle-based videos" (undersells the content)
- ❌ "Template by template" (removed from footer)
- ❌ Overly polished or corporate language

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Purpose | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary Background | Mint Green | `#C6F5D5` | Hero section, background, brand wall |
| Primary Text | Ink | `#0F0F0F` | Body text, headings, borders |
| Accent/Highlight | Coral/Pink | `#FF6B9D` | CTA buttons, links, accents |
| Secondary Accent | Sky Blue | `#62C3FF` | Marker color, highlights |
| Muted Text | Gray | `#6B7280` | Secondary text, disabled states |
| Paper/White | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, modals, backgrounds |

### Typography

- **Font Family:** Bricolage Grotesque (all weights)
- **Base Font Size:** 16px
- **Line Height:** 1.5 (base), 1.1 (headings)
- **Heading Font:** Bricolage Grotesque, variable weights
- **Body Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (or system font fallback)

### Logo Usage

- **15 Official Brand Logos:** Samsung, Rapido, Uber, Zoho, realme, Xiaomi, OPPO, Mamaearth, boAt, Noise, Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra, Meesho, vivo
- **Minimum Clear Space:** Equal to the height of the "U" in Bricolage Grotesque
- **Minimum Size:** 32px width for digital, 1 inch for print
- **Clear Space Rule:** No other visual elements within the clear space area
- **Color Restriction:** Use logos in their official colors; do not recolor
- **Background:** Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 against background

### Imagery Style

- **Portrait Style:** Floating avatar with subtle motion (hero section)
- **Content Photos:** Challenges, builds, travel, on-camera experiments
- **Color Treatment:** Mint background consistent with brand; accent colors for highlights
- **Icon Style:** Line icons, rounded corners, consistent stroke weight
- **Avoid:** Stock photos without original context; overly polished corporate imagery

## Voice Framework

### Persona

**Pranay Gupta** - Creator, experimenter, teacher

- Approachable and genuine
- Shares both successes and failures
- Empathetic to viewer challenges
- Never gatekeeps "secrets"

### Content Themes

1. **Challenge Experiments** - Testing what works, what doesn't
2. **Build Projects** - Creating something from scratch
3. **Travel & Adventure** - On-location content creation
4. **Behind-the-Scenes** - Honest look at production
5. **Viewer Q&A** - Answering community questions

### Prohibited Language

- ❌ "Built video by video, not template by template" (removed)
- ❌ "The person behind the uploads" (removed)
- ❌ Any language that misrepresents content type
- ❌ Overly salesy or promotional phrasing

## Asset Management

### Logo Files

- **Location:** `public/partners/`
- **Formats:** SVG for vector, PNG for raster
- **Naming Convention:** `{brand-name}.{svg|png}`
- **Current Files:** amazon, boat, flipkart, mamaearth, meesho, myntra, noise, oppo, rapido, realme, samsung,uber, vivo, zoho

### Image Guidelines

- **Hero Portrait:** PNG with transparent background preferred
- **Screenshots:** 1200×630px recommended for social sharing
- **Screen Recordings:** 1920×1080px max, optimized with WebP/AVIF
- **File Naming:** `descriptive-name@2x.{format}`

### Design Assets

- **Color Tokens:** `assets/design-tokens.json`, `assets/design-tokens.css`
- **Component Library:** shadcn/ui components where applicable
- **Typography:** Bricolage Grotesque web font (self-hosted or Google Fonts)
- **Spacing Scale:** 4px/8px/12px/16px/24px/32px system

## Brand Consistency Checklist

### Before Publishing Any Content:

- [ ] Colors match brand palette (mint background, ink text, accent highlights)
- ✅ Typography uses Bricolage Grotesque
- ✅ Logo usage follows clear space and color restrictions
- ✅ Voice tone matches "crazy ideas on camera" framework
- ✅ No removed phrases ("Built video by video...", "The person behind the uploads")
- ✅ Accessibility contrast ratios met (4.5:1 minimum)
- ✅ Mobile responsive at 375/768/1024/1440px
- ✅ No horizontal overflow at any breakpoint
- ✅ Reduced-motion preference respected
- ✅ All 15 brand logos displayed with proper alignment
- ✅ Analytics and partners sections clearly separated ("For Brands")

### Design Review Triggers:

- New hero section or page layout
- Addition of new brand partners
- Visual refresh or redesign
- Accessibility audit findings
- New content format or series
